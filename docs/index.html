Is the spread real, or is it luck on 24 items?

Every call runs at temperature 0, so a wording's score on these 24 items is exact rather than estimated. The question worth asking is whether the same wording would still look good on different items. Two tests answer it.

The two null columns come from 1,000 permutations that keep each item's difficulty fixed and shuffle only which wordings got it right. That null still produces a visible range, which is why the range on its own is not evidence. Reliability is the mean Spearman-Brown corrected correlation between a wording's score on one random half of the items and its score on the other half, over 200 splits.

What this is a sample of

  • 386 wordings survived generation, and 346 of them are in the distribution above. 413 of 462 wordings were judged equivalent to the reference by gemini-3-flash-preview; the rest are excluded here. Without that filter the range on qwen3:8b would read 100% rather than 100%, and the extra width would come from prompts that changed the question rather than the wording.
  • The wordings were written by gemini-3-flash-preview under 14 style directives crossed with 10 structural directives, all chosen by the author. They are a sample from that generator, not a random sample of how people write prompts.
  • 508 candidates were generated, 46 were rejected by a structural filter, and 0 were exact duplicates. Near-duplicates were kept, because thinning them would make the pool look more varied than the thing that produced it.
  • On entail, a model judge called 104 of 120 sampled wordings equivalent to the reference (87%), and 30 of 30 survived a round trip through Spanish still judged equivalent to their own original.
  • On mult, a model judge called 413 of 462 sampled wordings equivalent to the reference (89%), and 40 of 40 survived a round trip through Spanish still judged equivalent to their own original.
  • 24 items per wording. That is a small item set, which is why the spread is tested against a permutation null and a split-half reliability rather than presented on its own.
